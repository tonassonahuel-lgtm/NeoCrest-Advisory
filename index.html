A simple, no-nonsense process

We agree on goals from day one and measure progress at every stage.

  1. Discovery

    An initial conversation and review of your situation: where you are, what’s in the way, and what you want to achieve.

  2. Action plan

    We set priorities, owners, and realistic deadlines for the next 90 days.

  3. Ongoing coaching

    Regular sessions with concrete assignments in between, so what we agree on gets done.

  4. Measure and adjust

    We review results, correct course where needed, and set the next goal.

The NeoCrest Success Index

Motivation is hard to see. Progress shouldn’t be. Before we begin, we measure where your company stands on a 0 to 100 scale across four areas. We measure again at day 45 and day 90, so you see exactly what changed and what didn’t.

The Index is built on my own working method, applied the same way with every client. You get a written baseline, an agreed target, and a clear record of results.

Common questions

Who do you work with?

Owners, executives, and sales leaders of small and mid-sized companies who want a clearer strategy, a stronger team, and someone to hold the plan accountable.

What happens on the first call?

A free, no-obligation conversation about your business, your goals, and what is in the way. If we’re a good fit, I’ll outline how we would work together.

How is the Success Index measured?

Through short assessments for you and your team, plus the business numbers we agree on together, such as pipeline, conversion, and retention. The same method is used at day 0, day 45, and day 90.

Do you work in person or online?

Both, anywhere in the world. Online sessions run on Zoom, and in-person engagements take place at your location, with travel arranged in advance by email.

What happens after the first 90 days?

You decide. You can continue with a new plan and a new target, move to a lighter schedule, or stop with everything documented.
